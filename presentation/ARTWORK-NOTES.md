# StockSense presentation artwork

The cover is original AI-generated conceptual brand artwork, made with the built-in Image Generation tool. It depicts an inventory-inspired still life; it is not a photograph of a customer warehouse. The supplied `cover-art.png` is embedded in both presentation formats. The StockSense logo and product captures come from the application.

## Generation brief

Create a premium editorial 3D still-life background for a 16:9 hackathon pitch deck for an inventory management product, StockSense. No text, letters, logos or UI. Deep evergreen teal (#102f2d), ivory, sage and lime (#baf264). Keep the left 55% nearly empty with a subtle dark teal atmospheric gradient for later white slide text. On the right, arrange five cubic inventory parcels and a small warehouse shelf in frosted glass, matte ivory ceramic and sage paper, with lime tape and a connected lime floor path. Use soft dimensional studio lighting and realistic shadows. Keep the composition clean, with no robots, holographic UI or floating numbers. Objects should sit fully inside the right 45%, leaving space above and below.

## Presentation assets

- Cover: conceptual AI-generated art, reused on the closing slide.
- Product screenshots: genuine captures from an isolated StockSense demo workspace.
- Slide 5: four captured states of receipt RCV-2513. These show a real 24 kg receipt, changing the selected warehouse balance from 46 kg to 70 kg after manager approval. They are explicitly labeled as a captured walkthrough.
- Text, headings and tables in the PowerPoint are editable. Screenshots and cover artwork are raster images.
